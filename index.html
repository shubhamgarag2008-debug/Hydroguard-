About HydroGuard V3

HydroGuard is a student-developed flood early-warning concept. Administrative names come from an LGD-derived directory; weather is requested live where possible; estimated flood values remain clearly labelled until actual verified sensors and a trained prediction model are connected.

© 2026 HydroGuard • V3 Demonstration • Estimated values are not official emergency warnings.
